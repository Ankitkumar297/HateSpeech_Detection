Transparent AI

Model Architecture & Benchmark Metrics

Complete technical documentation of the machine learning algorithms, dataset structure, and pipeline.

01

Text Ingestion & Regex Clean

Converts raw input to lowercase, strips URLs, handles `@mentions`, preserves hashtags, and removes extraneous symbols.

02

NLTK Stopwords & Stemming

Filters out high-frequency noise words while applying SnowballStemmer("english") to reduce inflectional forms.

03

CountVectorizer Extraction

Constructs unigram + bigram (1, 2) sparse feature vectors capturing critical phrase combinations and contexts.

04

Multi-Class ML Classifier

Evaluates log-odds probabilities with balanced class weighting to assign calibrated confidence across 3 categories.

Logistic Regression (Primary)
Production Model

Multi-class Logistic Regression with Softmax probability calibration. Highly effective for high-dimensional sparse text vectors, delivering superior generalization on nuanced language.

Dataset & Classification Taxonomy
Class 0: Hate Speech

Language that attacks, denigrates, incites violence, or dehumanizes individuals based on protected attributes (race, religion, nationality, disability, gender, or sexual orientation).

Class 1: Offensive Language

Vulgar, profane, or hostile language that contains swear words or insults without expressing targeted bigotry against a protected minority group.

Class 2: Neutral Content

Everyday civil conversations, news articles, academic text, constructive feedback, and positive social media commentary.

About HateShield NLP

Understanding the engineering methodology and practical motivations behind this content moderation system.

Motivation & Approach

Online platforms often struggle to differentiate between casual coarse language (slang, venting, mild profanity) and targeted hate speech directed at identity groups.

This project follows the taxonomy established in NLP research (such as Davidson et al., 2017) to separate Hate Speech from Offensive Language and Neutral Discussions. By leveraging NLTK Snowball Stemmer with n-gram tokenization and multi-class Logistic Regression, the system achieves balanced precision while preserving explainability.
